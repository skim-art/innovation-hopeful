ABOUT

I am a student. I created this website because the available information about sustainability programs is not accessible, reliable, or cohesive enough.

I aim for each webpage on DITHER to be informative, accurate, and helpful. I’ve provided links on each webpage to the individual organizations’s sites so that you have an easy way to access further information.

I feel like so much discourse around the environment emphasizes on how people are not doing enough to help. My goal with this website is to emphasize how people can help, empowering the public to see how they can make a difference. You can reduce the amount of trash bags coming out of your household weekly; trust me.
