Books are simply the best. They're pathways to other worlds, pictures of quiet afternoons. They can be colorful or black type on a page, but regardless, books are valuable and deserve to be treated that way.

If you live in Houston and you've recently done a deep clean of your bookshelf and found yourself with a pile of books that you've aged out of or don't want anymore, you have several options:

    Books for Development: This stellar organization takes your donated books and distributes them in developing countries to promote literacy. Books can be any language, but books in English, African languages, and Spanish are the most valuable. There are several ways of donating:
      Dropoff at 3709 Polk St, Houston, TX 77003 (their sorting warehouse) on Saturday from 9am-12pm (it's very fast and easy!). Call ahead to let them know that you're coming. Create a Book Box - essentially, a mobile library that will be in Africa. It involves a cash payment to set it up and centers around a specific theme (books curated by you). Learn more here. "Large Donation Pick Up" - mostly from schools and libraries.
    Find out more about their mission and what they will or won't accept on their website.
