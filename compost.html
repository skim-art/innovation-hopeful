Compost, Compost, Compost!

You will do so much good

It does cost money - but it's worth it.

I cannot stress this enough: composting is one of the most beneficial things you can do for the environment and for yourself. It eliminates your food waste. It eliminates the guilt you may hold about throwing food away. Once the food is fully composted, it may come back full circle to fuel your garden, bolstering your flowers or vegetable plants.

If you live in Houston, you have several options:

    Zero Waste Houston: This composting service picks up your food waste weekly. For a residential subscription (🏡🪴😁), after pre-paying for a bucket ($25), it costs $10/month if you live inside the 610 loop, and $35/month if you live in the Woodlands or Bellaire outside of the 610 loop (read more about residential composting service options here). If you are a business looking to compost (📈☘️😄), find out more details here. Moonshot Houston: This composting service picks up your food waste weekly. You can do compost yourself, in your backyard. There are several methods to do this: to get an overview, go to this blog post from Sustainable(ish).
