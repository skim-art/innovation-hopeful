Bring to Staples!

It's free!

With your crayons, you expressed your creativity and delighted others with your drawings. Now they're worn down to a stub, but they can still be recycled into something new. You can give back in an extremely easy and effective way by dropping off your crayons at any Staples store. Staples, through their Writing Tools Recycling Program, will ship them to The Crayon Initiative at no charge to you!

The Crayon Initiative is an excellent nonprofit whose goal is to "bring happiness to children who are facing the stress of hospitalization by giving them the freedom that comes with creative play." By donating your crayons to this organization through Staples, you are both reducing waste in the landfill and unleashing creativity and happiness 🙂. Thank you for donating!

Learn more about what else you can recycle with Staples here. Learn more about the Crayon Initiative here.

You are helping

According to The National Crayon Recycle Program, crayons are made from a petroleum by-product called paraffin wax. By recycling your crayons, you reduce the risk of paraffin wax polluting the natural world (while this study was not done on crayons, paraffin wax residue has been determined to, "if discharged into the sea during tank cleaning or ballast unloading, pose a risk to marine resources or human health" [according to the International Maritime Organization's 2007 Version of the Annex II]. Read more in this article about paraffin waxes in the North-Western Mediterranean Sea.

Additionally, petroleum is a nonrenewable resource which takes a lot of energy to extract, so thank you for preserving the need to manufacture more crayons. Finally, thank you for bringing smiles to children's faces with your donation.
