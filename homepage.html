Become part of the circle

The ideal:

A circular economy. In a circular economy, disposed substances are not incinerated or dumped in a landfill. Instead, materials are reintroduced into production or kept out of the landfills through sustainability programs, primarily recycling, composting, and donation services.

The problem:

It takes a lot of effort to search for programs that recycle/compost/receive donations. The general public does not know enough about these programs that make it easy, and often free, for individuals/households to reduce their waste.

The solution:

My mission in creating this website is to help you, the consumer, find out more about sustainability programs that are accessible and affordable so that you can reduce your waste and become part of the circle.
