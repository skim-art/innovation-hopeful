🖍️ Crayons 🖍️

Bring to Staples!

It's free!

Your crayons helped you express your creativity and delight others with your drawings. Now they're worn down to a stub, but they can still be recycled into something new. You can give back in an extremely easy and effective way by dropping off your crayons at any Staples store. Staples, through their Writing Tools Recycling Program, will ship them to The Crayon Initiative at no charge to you!

The Crayon Initiative is an excellent nonprofit whose goal is to "bring happiness to children who are facing the stress of hospitalization by giving them the freedom that comes with creative play. By donating your crayons to this organization through Staples, you are both reducing waste in the landfill and unleashing creativity and happiness 🙂. Thank you for donating!

Learn more about what else you can recycle with Staples here. Learn more about the Crayon Project here.

☕️ Nespresso Pods ☕️

Mail to Nespresso!

It's free!

Coffee really does make some people's worlds go 'round. It helps them wake up and have a productive day, then go to sleep again planning to make a steaming cup once they wake up.

Reflecting that spirit of circularity, Nespresso has a program that's entirely free of charge for you to recycle Nespresso pods!

You can recycle your pods by

  1. Bag: Purchase coffee through the Nespresso website, and you can also add a free recycling bag to your order. Fill the recycling bag with used pods and ship it to Nespresso through UPS.
  2. Bin: If you live in New York or a low-rise building in New Jersey, you can put used pods directly in the recycling bin. Of course, check with building management to double check.
  3. Boutique: You can hand-deliver used Nespresso pods to any Nespresso "boutique," or store location.

    4. Learn more about Nespresso's vision and mission here.

    LEGOS

    Mail to LEGO to donate!

    It's free!

    LEGOs uncover your truest creativity. In fact, one of their only downsides is that once people are done with them, they often toss them in the trash. If you've aged out of certain sets or have too many minifigures lying around, do not throw them away!

    LEGO has a program called LEGO Replay. They take your bricks and send it through their donation networks, which gives the LEGOs a second opportunity to inspire more joy. Find out more about their reach here

    Donating does require a bit of work. You cannot take a completely or partially built set and mail it to LEGO; every piece must be individually separated. Breaking down your old sets can be a great weekend project! Learn more about what can be accepted or not here.

    Put the individual pieces in a box (make sure that you can carry heavy objects; over time, even though LEGO pieces are small, accumulated pieces become quite hard to lift). If you go on the LEGO website, there is a button where you can obtain a free shipping label. Send it off knowing that you made a difference for someone else's playtime and the planet's wellbeing 🙂.

    🍷 Wine Corks

    Recycle with Spec's!

    It's free! Save your corks.

    After a glass of red, white, or rosé, you know to recycle the glass bottle. But what about the wine cork?

    The wine company Spec's has partnered with Widget Club's sustainability initiative CorkClub to make recycling your wine corks easy. Simply bring your wine corks, synthetic or natural, to Spec's, and they will recycle it free of charge to you.

    You are not only easing strain on the landfill by recycling your corks. You are also contributing towards multiple causes.

      Spec's donates money through their Cork Program to the Texas Children's Cancer and Hematology Center to support cancer research. Learn more from Spec's website here. When CorkClub receives natural corks, they donate money towards forest and ocean conservation groups (synthetic corks are recycled). Learn more from WidgetCo's website here.

    Message for restaurant/club employees/employers

    Spec's will pick up your corks for free. Of course, you can donate by bringing it to a Spec's store location, but if Spec's delivers product to your location, they can also pick up corks during their delivery! Learn more about logistics here.

    Don't want to go to Spec's?

    You can also ship wine corks (up to 40 pounds or 120 corks) directly to Cork Club in a box. Disclaimer: this is not free. Learn more here.
