LEGOS

Mail to LEGO to donate!

It's free!

LEGOs uncover your truest creativity. In fact, one of their only downsides is that once people are done with them, they often toss them in the trash. If you've aged out of certain sets or have too many minifigures lying around, do not throw them away!

LEGO has a program called LEGO Replay. They take your bricks and send it to donation organizations in their network, which gives the LEGOs a second opportunity to inspire more joy. Find out more about their reach here.

Donating does require a bit of work. You cannot take a completely or partially built set and mail it to LEGO; every piece must be individually separated. Breaking down your old sets can be a great weekend project! Learn more about what can be accepted or not here.

Put the individual pieces in a box (make sure that you can carry heavy objects; over time, even though LEGO pieces are small, accumulated pieces become quite hard to lift). If you go on the LEGO Replay donation page, there is a button where you can obtain a free shipping label. Send it off knowing that you made a difference for someone else's playtime and the planet's wellbeing 🙂.

You are helping

According to the LEGO 2024 Sustainability Statement, LEGO produced 25,859 tonnes of waste from their manufacturing sites and 144,358 tCO2e (carbon emissions). Manufacturing LEGO bricks takes a lot of energy; by sending your LEGO bricks to the Replay program, you are making sure that that energy does not go to waste. You are also ensuring that children have LEGOs to play with and create with. Thank you for reducing waste and encouraging play.
