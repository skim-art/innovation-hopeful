☕️ Nespresso Pods ☕️

Mail to Nespresso!

It's free!

Coffee really does make some people's worlds go 'round. It helps them wake up and have a productive day, then go to sleep again planning to make a steaming cup in the morning.

Reflecting that spirit of circularity, Nespresso has a program that's entirely free of charge for you to recycle Nespresso pods!

You can recycle your pods by

  1. Bag: Purchase coffee through the Nespresso website, and you can also add a free recycling bag to your order. Fill the recycling bag with used pods and ship it to Nespresso through UPS (the bag is prepaid!).
  2. Bin: If you live in New York or a low-rise building in New Jersey, you can put used pods directly in the recycling bin. Of course, check with building management to double check.
  3. Boutique: You can hand-deliver used Nespresso pods to any Nespresso "boutique," or store location.

    4. Learn more about Nespresso's vision and mission here.

    You are helping

    Aluminum can be recycled an infinite amount of times (essentially). According to The Aluminum Association, "making recycled aluminum only takes around 5% of the energy needed to make new aluminum" and has the potential to save more than 1 billion dollars in the US economy each year just by recycling aluminum cans. By recycling aluminum, you are saving money and the environment. Thank you!
