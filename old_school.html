Physical Storage Media

Bring to Best Buy/Staples!

Not too old school to recycle

Physical media should be treasured while they're usable - and they should be valued in their afterlife. When they break down, you can bring them to Best Buy or Staples to recycle them!

Best Buy will accept the following items for recycling:

  • Blu-ray players
  • Cassette players
  • CD players/recorders
  • In-dash/overhead DVD players
  • Personal recorders
  • Any portable media players

  • NOTE: they will not accept discs.

Staples will accept the following items for recycling:

  • Blu-ray discs and players
  • CD discs and players
  • DVD discs and players

You are helping

E-waste, no matter if it's from modern or older tech, is a terrible problem. According to the United Nations Institute for Training and Research (UNITAR), e-waste levels are projected to reach 82 million tonnes by 2030. Disposing of electronics in the trash results in the valuable, still usable minerals within the phone being put to waste. E-waste often contains mercury, a dangerous element. Recycling electronics, UNITAR projects, could result in "a global net positive of US $38 billion" for the economy. It'll conserve important minerals and reduce the risk of leaching toxic substances into the environment. Thank you for doing your part!
