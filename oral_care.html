Oral Care Products

Recycle!

It's simple

In general, the oral care industry is not the most sustainable - plastic brushes, tiny pieces of plastic floss, and plastic toothpaste tubes are used up quickly and thrown away even quicker.

Luckily, Colgate has developed a completely recyclable toothpaste tube! These products are still being rolled out, so you will have to check whether or not your Colgate toothpaste tube can actually be recycled. Colgate is working on, as they term it, "sharing the technology and partnering with third parties to help turn 'recyclable' into widely accepted and 'recycled.'" Their new toothpaste tubes are made out of #2 plastic (tube) and #5 plastic (cap).

It's easy to tell if the Colgate toothpaste tube is recyclable - simply look for the How2Recycle label on the tube! If the label indicates that the tube's recyclable, go ahead and put it in your household recycling after squeezing out as much of the toothpaste as possible (you do not have to worry too much about excess toothpaste because the recycling process rinses the toothpaste off anyway). Learn more about Colgate's amazing initiative their website.

You're helping

The United Nations Environment Programme (in a beautifully illustrated graph on page 11 of this report) estimates that more than 291 million tonnes of plastic are wasted per year and only 62 million tonnes, or around 21 percent, is recycled per year. Thank you for increasing the amount of plastic that is recycled per year - and don't forget to smile! 😃
