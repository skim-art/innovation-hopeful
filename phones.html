Phones

Recycle or trade in!

Don't snooze

Everything about phones is customizable: your case, your wallpaper, your apps (games, social media), how much time you choose to spend staring at the screen. It turns out that how to dispose of your phone after it has run its course is customizable too.

You have several options. You can trade in the phone if the phone producer provides that option (here are links to Apple Trade In and Samsung Trade In). Apple also does electronics recycling for free - they provide a prepaid shipping label for you through their Trade In webpage (learn more about their program here).

This webpage will focus on phone recycling programs, which include the following:

    Eco-Cell: This amazing electronics recycling organization wants to keep electronics out of the landfill AND aims to preserve gorilla habitats. You can get your phone to them directly by mailing it to Eco-Cell (203 East Fairmont Ave, Louisville, KY 40214) OR you can drop them off at the Houston Zoo x Eco-Cell collection bin. 🦍 Staples: You can bring your phone (any brand) to Staples, and they will recycle it for free! Learn more on their website here. They can also recycle the cases (any brand)! Best Buy: You can bring your phone to Best Buy (any brand), and they will recycle it for free! Learn more on their website here. They cannot recycle the cases.

You are helping

If you recycle your phone with Eco-Cell, you are helping to preserve gorilla habitats!
