Construction Materials 🚥

Bring to the Reuse Warehouse!

It's free!

There is a lot of construction in Houston, all the time. In fact, the Houston City Government estimates that more than 1/3 of waste produced in Houston comes from construction/demolition materials and activity. Therefore, it is very important for organizations to be aware of Houston's Reuse Warehouse.

The City of Houston Building Materials Reuse Warehouse is a space where individuals/builders/supply companies can drop off excess building material. Then, nonprofits/schools/universities/government agencies can repurpose that building material, taking it from the Warehouse for free, to use for their own. While individuals are not allowed to take material directly from the Warehouse by themselves (for now), individuals can ask the organizations that can take building materials in order to procure what the individuals need. The warehouse does not limit how much people take!

The Reuse Warehouse is on 9003 N. Main Street, Houston, TX 77022. Find out more about it on this webpage. Find a concise video overview of its mission and function here.

You are helping.

Research from 2019 has shown, according to this paper, that C & D (Construction & Demolition) waste exceeds 10 billion tons every year, to which the United States contributes 700 million tons. Additionally, the construction industry "is not only considered to be the most important contributor to waste, but also the primary cause of environmental pollution" (source: this scientometric analysis. By dropping off material at the Reuse Warehouse, you are easing strain on the landfill and enabling a perfectly usable material to have a second life.
