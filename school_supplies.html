Writing Utensils, Glue, and More School Supplies

Bring to Staples!

It's free!

When you are a student, you are constantly surrounded by pencils, pens, highlighters, rulers, and all other matter of school supplies. The unfortunate thing is that these supplies do not last forever. Luckily, Staples has saved the day by creating a reycling program for basically all of the school supplies!

It's simple. Just collect the following items if they're unusable:

  • Pencils
  • Pens
  • Markers
  • Crayons
  • Colored pencils
  • Highlighters
  • Rulers
  • Backpacks
  • Lunch bags
  • Binders
  • Zipper Binders
  • Glue sticks & bottles (that are empty)
Bring the items to Staples, and they will recycle it for free!

You are helping

Earth 911 reports that 1.6 billion plastic pens are thrown away every year by the United States alone. Think about how many pencils, binders, or glue sticks you use. Think about the millions of pounds of waste that you could prevent by recycling. Thank you for recycling your products.
