🧴 Dove Shampoo Bottles 🧴

Recycle!

It's easy!

Perfect soap bubbles come in one of the best shapes: circles. Have you ever heard of a circular economy? According to A Circular Economy for Flexible Packaging, instead of the "linear economy," which has a "Take, Make, Dispose" mentality towards item production and disposal, circular economies try to recycle/reuse products to give them a second life.

Dove has done their part in starting to transition to circular products. Read more about their sustainability goals here on their website.

For now, while they are achieving their goals, Dove has partnered with creative platform Refinery29 to make an infographic about sustainable beauty. According to the poster, which you can view under the subtab "Conscious Beauty" here, plastic shampoo bottles can be recycled following these steps: "Empty, Rinse, Remove Any Pumps, Replace the Lid, Leave the Label On, Squash the Bottle."

As always, make sure to check your recycling service's guidelines. To double check, you can see whether the shampoo bottle's plastic number is accepted in the recycling service's facilities.
