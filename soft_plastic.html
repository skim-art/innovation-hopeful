(Most) Soft Plastic

Bring to H-E-B/Kroger/check your store...

A grocery and recycling run in one go!

H-E-B proudly proclaims their motto "No Store Does More." In terms of recycling, they're both right and wrong.

H-E-B has a wonderful program where they collect soft plastic through in-store dropoff. This plastic includes the type that makes up the plastic grocery bags that any store gives out. Collect your plastic grocery bags - don't throw them out after unpacking your groceries!. Bring the bags back to H-E-B and drop them off in the designated spot.

H-E-B can also recycle other types of plastic, notably produce bags, bread bags, and newspaper bags. Please, learn more about what H-E-B recycles on their website here.

So, H-E-B's committment to helping the environment is real. But another store that does "more" is Kroger.

To recycle with Kroger, simply drop off the following items in Kroger's Zero Hunger | Zero Waste Bins at the front of their stores:

  1. Plastic Grocery Bags
  2. Produce bags
  3. Bubble wrap
  4. CLean sandwich bags
  5. Bread bags
  6. Plastic overwrap from paper towels
  7. Cereal box liners
  8. Dry cleaning bags
  9. Newspaper sleeves
  10. Plastic shipping envelopes

Find out more about what can be recycled on their website (scroll all the way down for a helpful infographic).

Kroger also collaborated with Terracycle to create a free mail-in recycling program for waste from Kroger-branded products. These brands include Kroger®, Private Selection®, Simple Truth®/Simple Truth Organic™, HemisFares®, Comforts™, and Luvsome™.

Simply

  1. Make a Terracycle account through this link
  2. Select the shipping option you desire (FedEx or UPS)
  3. Ship the packaging to TerraCycle for free!

Whether you shop at H-E-B or Kroger, make sure to save your plastics that they recycle! Learn more about H-E-B's program here. Learn more about Kroger's program here.

You are helping

Since 2004, H-E-B has recycled over 58 million pounds of plastic! Kroger has recycled over 140 million pounds of plastic. Thank you for helping these positive statistics grow!
