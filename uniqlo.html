UNIQLO Products

Bring back to UNIQLO!

On your next shopping spree, save the planet - for free.

UNIQLO is a Japanese apparel brand known for their comfortable, stylish, and minimalist clothing. They have an awesome in-store collection program called RE.UNIQLO, in which you can bring any used UNIQLO item in any condition to a UNIQLO store and drop them off in a RE.UNIQLO bin. RE.UNIQLO will only accept UNIQLO products.

This program makes change in multiple ways.

  1. If the dropped off item are still usable, UNIQLO will send it to be donated.
  2. If the dropped off item is in bad condition, UNIQLO will send it to the textil recycling company Supercircle to be recycled.
  3. In terms of down jackets, UNIQLO collects the feathers/down from inside them and reuses the material for new down jackets

No matter what UNIQLO item you drop off, you are making a difference!

You are helping

In 2025, the United Nations Environment Programme made textile/fashion industry waste the focus of their International Day of Zero Waste 2025. According to their research, 92 million tonnes of textile waste is produced annually and globally. 11% of total plastic waste results from textiles. Textile waste is dumped in landfills or burned, frequently in low-income countries, resulting in awful environmental conditions. Thank you for recycling your clothing and choosing to not contribute to these numbers. Read more from the UNEP's press release here.
