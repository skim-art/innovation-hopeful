Metal Waterbottles

You're in luck if you have a Hydroflask, Stanley, or Yeti Rambler!

Bring to the respective locations!

When you use metal waterbottles, you are already helping the planet since you are not using plastic waterbottles. However, you can go further once your waterbottle is too dented or scratched up to serve its purpose anymore.

Three prominent waterbottle companies have trade-in/recycling programs: Hydroflask, Stanley, and Yeti.

    Hydroflask:

    You can recycle any stainless steel Hydroflask product - it can only be products from Hydroflask! If there are additional parts to the product (such as caps and lids) that are not reusable, you can send those in too. Stickers can be on the product - you don't have to peel them off.

    To recycle, all you have to do is clean the product, go to the Trade In webpage and press the button "Start Your Trade In >>". Follow the subsequent instructions, and a FedEx shipping label will be sent to your email for you to ship the amount of items (up to 10 per shipment) that you are sending to Hydroflask.

    Once your item is received and accepted, Hydroflask will award you $5 (100 loyalty points) towards your next Hydroflask order!

    Learn anything else you desire to know on the Trade In webpage

     Stanley:

    You can recycle any stainless steel Stanley product + straws/lids - it can only be products from Stanley! If you have additional questions, look at this FAQ on the Stanley website.

    To recycle, clean the product, go to the the Take Back webpage and click the button "Start Take Back". Follow the subsequent instructions, and you will get an option to either print a UPS shipping label or find UPS locations to dropoff your Stanley item. You can ship up to four Stanley items per year.

    Once Stanley receives and accepts your item, Stanley will give you a $5 Stanley 1913 credit AND will donate $5 towards ocean conservation 🌊.

    For more information, visit the Take Back webpage.

     Yeti (Rambler Drinkware):

    Through Yeti's Rambler Buy Back program, you can recycle any Yeti Rambler Drinkware products - up to 10 per dropoff - by visiting Yeti store locations.

    First, clean the product thoroughly, go to the Yeti Rambler Buy Back webpage, and click the button "Find a Store". The Buy Back program works with in-store dropoff. If one of the stores on that list is near you and a viable location to visit, you're in luck!

    In return for your item(s), Yeti will give you $5 per item that you drop off towards your next in-store purchase, meaning you could potentially exchange for up to $50!

    For more information and a look at the FAQs, click here for the Yeti Rambler Buy Back webpage

You're helping

Stainless steel is already an amazingly eco-friendly material due to its capacity to be infinitely recycled, according to the study "Traits and Characteristics of Eco Sustainable Stainless Steel 304". By sending your stainless steel waterbottles to these programs, you are using stainless steel's sustainable property to your advantage rather than just throwing your waterbottle in the landfill.

Additionally, recycling stainless steel lessens the need for companies to mine more metals that compose stainless steel, saving energy. The 2008 study "The energy benefit of stainless steel recycling" found that stainless steel recycling in 2004 "reduce[d] energy use by 33% . . . and CO2 emissions by 32% (29 million tons)."

Thank you for being a part of the change.
